## Product scope

Build a mobile-friendly campus second-hand marketplace with Flask, HTML/CSS, Jinja, MongoDB, and PyMongo. The first version covers accounts, browsing, item details, publishing, searching, editing, deletion, and marking items sold. Use image URLs initially; payment and instant messaging are deferred.

The plan groups the interface into seven page types: login/registration, browsing, item detail, publishing, My items, editing, and search results. Browsing and searching are public. Publishing and management require login, and users may modify only their own listings.

## Proposed shared contracts

- Item fields: `title`, `description`, `price`, `category`, `condition`, `image_url`, `contact_info`, `owner_id`, `status`, `created_at`, and `updated_at`.
- The backend supplies the seller ID, initial status, and timestamps.
- Search parameters: `q`, `category`, `min_price`, and `max_price`. Filters combine, results retain selected filters, and public results show active items by default.
- Share the database connection, current-user information, navigation/CSS, item cards, listing form, and category/condition/status options.

## Planned integration and checks

First establish common fields, choices, routes, parameters, and basic styling. Build the foundation, cards, form, management flow, and search layout in parallel. Merge a minimal version that starts and connects to MongoDB, provide sample items, then connect each feature to real data and integrate account-dependent flows.

The proposed walkthroughs are:

1. Log in, publish an item, find it in browsing and search, then open its detail page.
2. Edit it and check detail/search updates, then mark it sold or delete it and verify public results change.
